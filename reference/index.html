Isometric puzzles

Line: drag from one grid point to another. Fill: pick a tone, then tap inside a face closed by your lines. Tones: top faces light, front faces (lower right) medium, side faces (lower left) dark. The blue hexagon is the box that holds the shape. Answer shows the solution over your drawing. Check marks your lines: green = correct, red = extra, orange dashes = missing. Only lines count, not fills. You can draw the shape anywhere on the grid. Your drawing is kept in this browser.
Which net folds into this shape?
How the correct net folds · blue square = bottom, it stays on the table
Solid line = cut. Dashed line = fold. All folds are 90°, and you may fold the paper either way. Tip: pick one square as the bottom, then fold the squares around it in your head, one at a time. Wrong nets have one square in the wrong place, so look for two squares that would land on the same face.
